DXBC checksum in XeniOS (XeniaCore)

XeniaCore links XeniOS's third_party/dxbc (DXBCChecksum.cpp), an adaptation
by Xenia of AMD's DXBC checksum from GPUOpen-Archive/common-src-ShaderUtils
(DX10/DXBCChecksum.cpp). The two files are not identical: XeniOS's copy
(cf7c7ee452314913124d205e41e325b33891aacb, 16,615 bytes, SHA-256
73279b79725ea61ec1a04a059a9d954e0573a630491c475bf9b2aecd002cb29b) differs from
AMD's (545195d74ff0758f74ebf9a8b4c541f9390a1df7, 16,502 bytes, SHA-256
b643a548a60966b28bce280a3a9acf5961d2ae63dd28a68a72288dbe18085462).

Its MD5 code is derived from the RSA Data Security, Inc. MD5 Message Digest
Algorithm.

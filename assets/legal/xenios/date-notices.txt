Howard Hinnant's date library in XeniOS (XeniaCore)

XeniaCore compiles XeniOS's src/xenia/base/chrono.h, which includes
third_party/date (https://github.com/HowardHinnant/date, submodule commit
f94b8f36c6180be0021876c4a397a054fe50c6f2 at XeniOS
5533fbfd19d45f32b40283de2382e7f54248bea9): include/date/date.h and
include/date/tz.h. XeniOS's Acknowledgements.json does not list it. Each file
carries its own MIT notice (the project's LICENSE.txt, shipped beside this
file as date-LICENSE.txt, says so); the two notices are reproduced below as
published.

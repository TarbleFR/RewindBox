# RewindBox Glacier — interface and System Art attribution

RewindBox embeds the **RewindBox Glacier** presentation (menu background,
navigation glyphs and the `rewindbox-glacier` System Art pack, version 1.0.0)
so it can be installed and used offline.

- Visual direction based on the RewindBox banner and the approved menu mock-up
  supplied by the project. The emblem is the official RewindBox emblem.
- The 121 system portraits are studio illustrations created with AI assistance,
  then cut out and encoded as WebP. They are artistic interpretations of the
  hardware, not official photographs. Software systems and collections are
  shown as symbolic objects. The artwork does not declare or enable any
  emulated platform: availability is decided by RewindBox's own catalogue.
- For the references listed in the pack's `credits-silhouettes.json`, BAXY /
  baxysquare "Systematic" views guided shapes and proportions. The delivered
  illustrations are new interpretations (perspective, lighting and materials
  differ); no third-party file is redistributed.

    https://github.com/baxysquare/baxy-retroarch-themes/tree/3798875e3a592b2b5d737212be3a2d4bc9454629
    (BAXY Themes are licensed under the MIT License,
    https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
  - Libretro retroarch-assets — Systematic renders:
    https://github.com/libretro/retroarch-assets/tree/d9f969054dc7fbb6fa89519036d2b971e0855b51/xmb/systematic

- Console, service and product names identify the systems. Trademarks belong

The full pack credits ship with the application at
`assets/system_art/rewindbox-glacier/` (`CREDITS.md`, `credits-silhouettes.json`,

RetroAchievements disc reader in the libretro bridge (NEOSTATION_RA_DISC_READER_V1)

packages/libretro_internal_bridge/ios/Classes/RBDiscHashReader.c lets rcheevos
identify .chd and .cso images. The libretro bridge compiles, for that, the
pinned copy of libchdr in packages/libretro_internal_bridge/ios/ThirdParty/libchdr
(upstream https://github.com/rtissera/libchdr at
47d794151b2b64f6aba60304e6480f4f5a2bc24b) with two of its bundled dependencies:
the LZMA SDK decoder (deps/lzma-26.02) and the single-file Zstandard decoder
(deps/zstd-1.5.7/zstddeclib.c). Its header include/dr_libs/dr_flac.h is
reached by libchdr. The texts below are reproduced as published; the source
copy is described in ThirdParty/libchdr/NEOSTATION_NOTICE.md.
